One-line office joke for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open" bounty. The program writes a single original joke (114 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Our office fridge has a cleaning schedule, a sign-up sheet, and one lunchbox from 2023 that has seniority over me.

Re-entry after round 2 (the previous standup line slipped to 603/3016): twelve fresh candidates were written and compared pairwise against the judge's published criteria in `CANDIDATES.md`; this fridge line won on its end-loaded turn, concrete office detail, and a setup none of the entries ranked just above used.
